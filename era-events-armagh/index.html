Where it started · 9 August 2025

30 years of dance classics

The first ERA turned the Shambles Yard into a dancefloor for the first time. The idea was simple: bring back the nights out we all remember, with the DJs who soundtracked them, in the middle of our own city.

The headliners brought their own anthems with them. If you were out in the 90s, you know every one of these.

The venue

The Shambles Yard

A council review named the Shambles the most suitable outdoor event venue in the borough. It sits in the city centre, a short walk from bars, restaurants and places to stay.

Full production

A proper stage, full lighting and FX rig, and a sound system built for the anthems.

Licensed bar

Fully licensed on site, run to the council’s licence conditions.

Local food

Food traders from the Food Heartland, so the menu is as Armagh as the crowd.

Safe and looked after

A licensed security team on site from the moment the gates open.

Home by ten

Saturdays finish at 10pm and Sundays at 9pm. The night out without the headache.

Easy to reach

City centre location with car parks, taxis and the bus station nearby. Book a room and make a weekend of it.

Good to know

Questions

Who is ERA for?

ERA is built for the over-30s: anyone who remembers the 90s and 00s club nights and wants that feeling back, without the 4am finish.

What time does it start and finish?

Saturdays run 4pm to 10pm and Sundays 4pm to 9pm. Check your ticket for last entry, which was 6pm at the 2026 Dance Classics day.

Where do I buy tickets?

Tickets are sold online through Glistrr. Join the ERA list above and we’ll email you the moment 2027 tickets go on sale.

Is there food and a bar?

Yes. There’s a fully licensed bar and food traders from the local Food Heartland on site.

Where should I stay?

The Shambles Yard is in Armagh city centre, within walking distance of hotels, guesthouses and B&Bs. Book early for bank holiday weekends.

Can I book ERA for a private event?

Yes. Use the private hire form above and the team will get back to you.
